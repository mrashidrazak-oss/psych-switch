Privacy

Last updated 2026-05-08

PsychSwitch is privacy-first. The app does not require an account, does not transmit clinical inputs to any server, and does not track its users.

What stays on your device

  • Patient context (age band, renal/hepatic, comorbidities) — stored as parameters, never as a patient record.
  • Saved cases (your own free-form labels and switch parameters).
  • Reviewer sign-offs and starred cases.
  • Notification schedule index (per saved case).
  • App-level preferences (theme, text size, reminder time).
  • Onboarding seen flag.

What is sent off your device

  • Crash reports — anonymous, stack traces only, opt-in via Settings → Privacy. Off by default. Never includes clinical inputs.
  • Over-the-air updates — pulled from Expo on app start. One-way: server to device. No data uploaded in the request.
  • Only when you tap "Share schedule": the formatted plan goes to whichever app you choose (WhatsApp, email, etc.). PsychSwitch sees no copy.

What we never collect

  • Patient name, NRIC, MRN or DOB.
  • Location, contacts, photos, calendar.
  • Browser history or cross-app fingerprinting.
  • Analytics on which rules you view or which patients.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally-stored data. From within the app: Settings → Reset → "Clear all local data" wipes patient context, saved cases, sign-offs, notification schedules and preferences in one tap.

Verifying these claims

The app source is open at github.com/mrashidrazak-oss/psych-switch. Search the repo for any networking / analytics primitive — you won't find any except the Expo OTA download endpoint and the optional Sentry DSN (which is gated by your opt-in).
